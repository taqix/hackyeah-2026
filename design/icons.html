Movo · brand

App icon

The chosen direction: Monitor. A heartbeat line whose beat is the M of Movo, fading in from the left, with a bright o at its tip as if it is being drawn right now. The line uses the design system's blue.

Movo

The beat is an M. The dot is the o, and it is where the line is being drawn, so the mark always reads as "now": you, moving, today. The fade on the left keeps the start quiet; only the effort and the present are bright.

Versions

Default on warm ink. Dark uses the night background. Tinted is the one-colour version iOS tints and Android uses for its monochrome icon.

Sizes

App Store, home screen, Spotlight and Settings sizes, plus the favicon.

On a home screen

Export

  • Export the default at 1024 × 1024 with no rounded corners; the system applies the mask.
  • iOS: add the dark and tinted versions to apps/mobile/assets/expo.icon.
  • Android adaptive icon: background #1D1914 (set adaptiveIcon.backgroundColor in app.json), the line and dot as the foreground inside the centre 66 %, and the tinted glyph as the monochrome layer. Monochrome layers cannot fade, so there the line is solid.
  • Reuse the glyph for the splash screen and the web favicon.
